    Methodology

    Here is how each number on the dashboard is calculated. Σ (sigma) means “add up the results for every asset”.

    Portfolio allocation

    Asset investment ÷ Total portfolio investment × 100

    The share of your total money sitting in one asset. If ₹20,000 of a ₹45,000 portfolio is in one fund, that fund is 44.44% of the portfolio.

    Weighted portfolio return

    Σ (Asset weight × Asset return)

    The portfolio's overall return, where bigger investments count for more. An asset's weight is its allocation written as a decimal (44.44% = 0.4444). A ₹40,000 asset moves the result far more than a ₹4,000 one, which is why this is not a simple average.

    Estimated gain

    Σ (Asset investment × Asset return ÷ 100)

    The rupee profit (or loss) if every asset delivers its return once. Work out the gain on each asset, then add them together. A negative return gives a loss.

    Equity allocation

    Total equity investment ÷ Total investment × 100

    The percentage of your money in assets marked Equity. Add up all the Equity amounts, then divide by the total portfolio.

    Debt allocation

    Total debt investment ÷ Total investment × 100

    The same idea for assets marked Debt. Anything marked Other makes up the remaining share, so Equity, Debt and Other always add up to 100%.

    Returns are applied once and are not compounded or annualized. The figures are only as accurate as the returns you enter.
